THE HARMONIC ARCHIVE

The palettes

Four seasons. Endless expressions of you.

Warm • Light • Clear

SPRING

Spring colors are luminous, warm, and radiant. Inspired by blooming blossoms, warm golden sunlight, and fresh greenery. They illuminate skins with golden or peachy undertones.

CLOTHING

Coral, Peach, Warm Beige, Cream, Bright Teal

MAKEUP

Peach Nude, Warm Coral Lip, Warm Bronze

METALS

Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, Warm Brass

Cool • Light • Soft

SUMMER

Summer shades are muted, cool, and soft like sea mist and lavender fields. Gentle blues, powdery pinks, and dusty slates soothe and complement delicate cool skin undertones.

CLOTHING

Dusty Rose, Slate Blue, Soft Sage, Lavender

MAKEUP

Cool Mauve, Rose Tint, Soft Berry Lip

METALS

Polished Silver, Platinum, White Gold

Warm • Deep • Soft

AUTUMN

Autumn colors bring depth, warmth, and grounded richness. Terracotta, burnt amber, olive green, and warm espresso create an effortless sophisticated atmosphere for warm rich complexions.

CLOTHING

Terracotta, Olive, Burnt Amber, Warm Camel

MAKEUP

Spiced Brick Lip, Warm Bronze, Cinnamon

METALS

Antique Brass, Copper, Deep Yellow Gold

Cool • Deep • Clear

WINTER

Winter colors possess high clarity, deep intensity, and striking contrast. Vivid emeralds, royal blue, deep ruby, and obsidian black bring high-fashion distinction to cool high-contrast skin.

CLOTHING

Sapphire, Ruby Red, Emerald, Pure White, Black

MAKEUP

Deep Plum, Cool Crimson Lip, True Red

METALS

High-Shine Platinum, Sterling Silver

THE LUMÉA PHILOSOPHY

Your colors, understood differently.

A precise synthesis of undertone, value, and chromatic harmony.

01

OBSERVE

We start by examining your natural pigments in clear daylight conditions, setting aside environmental shadows or artificial indoor casts.

02

SELECT

Using our precision loupe color sampling interface, you pinpoint the exact hexadecimal tones of your skin undertone, natural hair highlights, and iris depth.

03

ANALYZE

Our rule-based color engine calculates the three core dimensions: Warmth (Golden vs. Rosy), Depth (Light vs. Deep), and Chroma (Clear vs. Soft).

04

DISCOVER

Your personal seasonal color family is revealed alongside a tailored horizontal swatch palette and tonal guidelines.

05

EXPLORE

Test individual wardrobe shades, makeup hues, and jewelry metals in real time with our Fashion & Makeup Color Checkers.

Note on Methodology: Luméa deliberately utilizes manual user-guided color sampling rather than automatic facial recognition AI. This ensures privacy, tactile accuracy, and a deeper personal awareness of your own unique coloring.
